Straight answers

Health decisions deserve plain language. Here is exactly how Pol handles your doctor, your money and your information.

Every doctor is verified before their first booking

We check each physician's medical licence and specialist credentials by hand. Their training and years of practice are on their profile so you can judge for yourself before booking.

You know the price, and you can get it back

The fee appears before you confirm — no consultation charges, no surprise add-ons. Cancel more than 24 hours before your visit for a full refund; cancel later and half is returned.

Every payment is matched to your booking by a person on our team.

Your visit stays between you and your doctor

Your notes and history are visible only to the doctor you booked and the small team that runs bookings. We don't sell data and we don't share your file with family members unless you ask us to.

We tell you when we're the wrong option

A video consultation can't examine you, order lab work in Canada, or write a Canadian prescription. If your case needs hands-on care, the doctor will say so and you can ask for a refund.

Pol is not for emergencies. If you have chest pain, trouble breathing, heavy bleeding, or thoughts of harming yourself, call 911 or go to your nearest emergency department.

Why Pol exists

Describing a symptom in a second language is its own kind of exhausting. You simplify. You leave out the part that seemed embarrassing. You nod when you didn't follow. For a parent who arrived later in life, it can mean skipping the appointment altogether.

Pol exists so that conversation can happen in Farsi, with a specialist who understands the culture around the question as well as the question itself — and at a price that doesn't make you think twice about asking.

We're new, and we'd rather say that than invent reviews. Pol is starting with patients in Canada and a small group of doctors we've verified one by one. If you use it early, tell us what broke — we'll fix it.
